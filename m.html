1 · Before you climb1 · 上场之前

1.1Gear — choosing climbing shoes装备：选攀岩鞋

  • Size: beginners should go half a size to one size up, or use their street-shoe size. Your toes should touch the front of the shoe comfortably, without intense pain.
  • Why not buy extremely tight shoes? Extreme tightness is for high-precision edging by advanced climbers. Beginners do not yet have that small-foot-muscle strength, and painful shoes will just make you want to quit.
  • What the shoes do: climbing shoes use sticky rubber that gives far more friction than regular sneakers, so you can stand on holds that look impossible in ordinary shoes.
  • 尺码：初学者选大半码到一码，或平日鞋码。脚趾碰到鞋头即可，不要剧痛。
  • 为什么不要买特别紧的鞋？极致贴合是给进阶者做精确踩点用的。初学者脚部小肌群还不够，痛只会让你想放弃。
  • 鞋的作用：攀岩鞋的橡胶摩擦力远高于普通运动鞋，才能踩住看起来不可能的点。

1.2Grades and color分级与颜色

  • American bouldering grades: V0 to V17. The number is only a starting point for how hard a problem feels.
  • European bouldering grades: 4a to 9a.
  • If you are a beginner: start at V0 and add difficulty slowly. Do not jump grades just because a color looks easy.
  • Color vs. grade: holds of the same color are one problem. That color is mapped to a grade, but every gym has its own color chart on the wall — read that chart before you pick a line.
  • 美式抱石等级：V0 到 V17。数字只是难度入口，手感还看点、距离和坡度。
  • 欧式抱石等级：4a 到 9a。
  • 初学者：从 V0 开始，慢慢往上加。不要因为某条颜色看起来简单就跳级。
  • 颜色与等级：同一种颜色是一条线路，颜色会对应等级。每个岩馆有自己的颜色说明图，选线前先看图。

1.3How to start and finish如何开始和结束

  • Read the start tags. Look for tape or labels on the holds. If there is one tag on a hold, both hands must start there. If there are two tags on different holds, one hand goes on each. Some gyms also tag specific starting footholds.
  • To begin, both feet leave the ground from that start.
  • Finish: match both hands on the Top hold and keep them there, stably, for two to three seconds — or tap the top marker, if that is how the gym marks a send.
  • 先看起步标记。找岩点上的胶带或标签。一个点上只有一枚标记：双手都要放在这个点。两个点上各有标记：一手一个。有的岩馆还会标起步脚点。
  • 开始时，双脚离开地面。
  • 结束：双手同时抓稳终点（Top），保持两到三秒；或按该馆规则轻拍顶部标记。

1.4Landing safely安全落地

  • Land on both feet: keep your feet about shoulder-width apart. That gives you a stable base and makes it less likely you will twist an ankle.
  • Bend your knees: let your knees and hips flex the moment you hit the pad. They are the shock absorbers. Do not keep your legs stiff.
  • Tuck your arms: keep your arms close to your chest, like a bear hug. Do not reach out to catch yourself with your hands — that is how wrists get broken.
  • Roll backward: let your momentum carry you into a smooth roll onto your back and shoulders, so leftover energy leaves the body instead of jamming into one joint.
  • 双脚落地：两脚大约与肩同宽，底盘更稳，脚踝不容易扭。
  • 弯曲膝盖：一碰到垫子，膝盖和髋马上屈曲，当作减震。不要把腿绷直。
  • 收臂：手臂靠近胸口，像熊抱。不要伸手去撑地——手腕很容易骨折。
  • 向后滚：顺着惯性滚到后背和肩膀，把剩余力量散掉，而不是砸进某一个关节。

1.5Mindset心态

Bouldering is a dialogue with yourself, not a competition against the person on the next problem.

You are solving a physics puzzle: gravity, the wall, and your own body. Everyone’s body is different, so everyone’s beta (the sequence that works) is different. Copying someone taller or stronger is optional, not a rule.

Know when to quit. If your forearms are pumped, or the drop feels unsafe, come down. Safety always comes first. Getting slightly better, slowly, is the whole game.

攀岩是跟自己对话，不是和旁边那个人比赛。

你在解一道物理题：重力、岩壁、自己的身体。每个人体型不同，过法（beta）也不同。可以看别人，但不必照抄更高或更壮的人。

及时放弃。前臂泵死了，或掉落觉得不安全，就下来。安全永远第一。缓慢进步，就是这件事本身。

1.6Before the real climbing正式攀爬前

  • Nails: check your fingernails and trim them. Long nails snag on holds and tear.
  • Pockets: empty your pockets. Keys, phones, and bottles hurt you — and whoever is under you — if you fall.
  • Warm up: always warm up before you climb. Cold fingers and shoulders are easy to injure.
  • Read the wall: before you pull on, look at the problem — where it goes, where you will land if you fall. Do not climb directly above or below someone else.
  • Pad etiquette: when you finish, step off the mats. Do not walk across the pads; they are a landing zone, not a corridor.
  • 指甲：先检查，最好修剪。长指甲容易卡在岩点上，把自己撕伤。
  • 口袋：不要装东西。钥匙、手机、水瓶掉下去会伤到自己，也会伤到下面的人。
  • 热身：爬之前一定要热身，避免手指和肩关节损伤。
  • 看线：上手前先观察线路走向、掉落会落在哪。不要爬到别人正上方或正下方。
  • 礼仪：结束后离开垫子。最好不要在垫子上走来走去——垫子是落地用的，不是过道。

2 · First time on the wall2 · 第一次上墙，重建发力

2.1Getting on the wall: like climbing stairs上墙：像走楼梯

  • Route: start with the easiest grade in the gym (V0–V1).
  • Before you pull on: look where it goes, and where you will land if you fall. Keep the landing zone clear.
  • On the wall: think with your feet. Move like walking up stairs — legs push you up; hands only keep you on and keep you balanced.
  • Coming down: practice getting off the wall safely, not only climbing up.
  • 选线：从馆里最简单的等级开始（V0–V1）。
  • 上手前：看线往哪走、掉了会落在哪。垫子上不要有人。
  • 上了墙：把注意力放在脚上。像走楼梯——腿把人推上去，手只负责挂住和保持平衡。
  • 下来：反复练习怎么安全下来，不要只会往上爬。

2.2Why do you get so tired so quickly?为什么这么快就累了

On the ground you grip tight, pull in, muscle through. On a vertical wall that same instinct burns you out.

在陆地上，直觉是抓紧、靠拢、用力。上了垂直的墙，这套动作会让身体很快僵掉。

  1. Hands aren't for pulling up. Feet are the engine. 手不是用来往上拉的

    The Trap:you try to pull up like a pull-up. A few moves later the forearms are pumped.

    反直觉点：一上墙就像做引体向上，没几步小臂泵血，胳膊发硬。

    The Fix:legs are the elevator. Each move is a squat: push off the feet. Hands only hang on and keep you balanced.

    需要培养：腿发力站起来。手只是把你挂在墙上。每一次移动都像蹲起，把人推上去，不是拉上去。

  2. Hips to the wall, not your chest. 贴墙的是胯

    The Trap:chest and belly glued on, like a frog. That pulls your weight out, so the arms hang you.

    反直觉点：胸口和肚子往墙上贴，整个人像只青蛙，重心其实已经被拽出墙外了，手在吊着你。

    The Fix:press the hips in. Turn sideways or open the hip so the weight sits over the feet. Closer hips, lighter hands.

    需要培养：把胯往墙上压。侧身、开胯都可以，让重量落到脚上面。胯越近墙，手越轻。

  3. Straight arms beat a bent lock-off. 手臂要打直

    The Trap:you bend the elbows and shrug, locking yourself close. The muscles never switch off.

    反直觉点：怕掉就弯肘、耸肩，人锁得很近，肌肉却一直在较劲。

    The Fix:when you're not moving, hang on straight, relaxed arms — like two ropes. Let the skeleton take the weight.

    需要培养：先别动态的时候，手臂放松打直，让骨头去挂体重。肌肉能歇就歇。

  4. Trust a tiny foothold. Put your weight on it. 小脚点也要敢踩

    The Trap:you hunt for big ledges, or smear without committing. A small nub looks like it will slip.

    反直觉点：只想踩大坑，或者拿鞋底去蹭。看见小突起就觉得会滑。

    The Fix:place the toe, press straight down. A thumbnail of rubber can hold you if you actually stand on it.

    需要培养：鞋尖踩准，把重心压上去。很多点只有指甲盖大，但只要压住，是站得住的。

  5. Relaxing is harder than pulling. 会放松比会发力难

    The Trap:everything switches on at once.

    反直觉点：一紧张全身一起绷，不该用力的时候也在耗。

    The Fix:shake out one hand, breathe; move, then find the next rest.

    需要培养：抓到好点就甩手、换气。要动就果断动，到位了立刻找下一个能歇的位置。

2.3Physics that changes the move用物理改动作

Leverage and torque — the “open book”

杠杆与力矩：对抗「开门」

Gravity is trying to rotate you off the wall. That rotation is torque. When one hand reaches out, the body wants to peel open like a book. A drop-knee or a hip turn brings the centre of mass in, shortens r, and takes load off the arms and back.

重力一直想把你转离墙，这个转的力就是力矩。一只手够远时，人会像书一样被掀开。侧身或 drop knee 把重心收回来，缩短 r，背和手臂就轻很多。

Action and reaction

作用与反作用

The wall does not lift you. It only gives back the force you put in. On an overhang the foot has to push in, or there is no friction. Direct that push through the skeleton, not through a pumped forearm.

墙不会把你托上去，只把你压进去的力推回来。外倾墙上，脚要往里压，才会有摩擦。让这股回力走骨头，不要走已经泵死的小臂。

Inertia and acceleration

惯性与加速度

Force makes acceleration; speed makes distance. A moving body reaches holds a still body cannot. Chain it: toe push → leg drive → hip swing → one-hand catch. Do not muscle the gap with the biceps.

F = ma。有速度才有距离。运动中的身体可以够到静止时够不到的点。连续加速：脚尖推 → 腿 → 髋摆 → 单手够。远点不要纯靠手拉。

2.4What actually saves energy怎样更省力

1. Stack the skeleton

1. 把骨头叠起来

  • Toe: like a ballet point — small contact, a clean upward push, less work for the arms.
  • Straight arms: a bent elbow is a new fulcrum, so the biceps fight gravity until they pump.
  • Hips in: shortens the lever. Same body, smaller pull at the hands.
  • 脚尖：像芭蕾踩点，接触面小，往上的力干净，手臂更轻松。
  • 手臂打直：弯肘会多出一个支点，二头肌一直在跟重力较劲，然后泵死。
  • 髋贴墙：缩短力臂。同样的体重，手上要拉的力更小。

2. Static balance

2. 墙上的平衡

  • Two-point, opposite side: right hand + left foot resists the open-book rotation. Same-side (left hand + left foot) opens the door.
  • Three-point: two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand — a triangle. The free limb is the one that moves.
  • 两点、异侧：右手+左脚更能顶住开门。同侧（左手+左脚）更容易被转出去。
  • 三点：两手一脚，或两脚一手，成一个三角。空出来的那只手脚去够下一个点。

3. Five seconds before you move

3. 移动前的五秒

  • Which way does the next hold face? Match the hand to the orientation.
  • If you let that hand go, is what remains a triangle — or a door?
  • If a door: high-step or switch feet first. Then push with the legs and use that momentum to the next hold.
  • 下一个点开口朝哪？哪只手更顺。
  • 松掉这只手之后，剩下的是三角，还是开门？
  • 若是开门：先上高脚或换脚。然后蹬腿，用惯性够到下一个点。

3 · Handholds and footwork3 · 抓法与脚法

3.1Handholds岩点与抓法

  • Jug: a big, positive hold. Wrap fingers and palm around it — the standard grip.
  • Pinch: squeeze the hold between the thumb and the other four fingers, like a volume or a rail.
  • Crimp: fingertips on a small edge, first knuckle flexed. Beginners’ tendons are fragile — use this cautiously.
  • Sloper: an open-hand on a rounded surface. Keep the pinky side of the palm close to the wall; this is the kindest grip for tendons.
  • Pocket: two or three fingers in a hole. Mono is a one-finger pocket — easy to injure. Jib: a tiny chip; often a foot, sometimes a desperate hand.
  • Jug 深点：好抓的大点。手指和手掌包住，就是标准的握。
  • Pinch 捏点：拇指对另外四指，把体积块或横条捏住。
  • Crimp 抠点：指尖抠小沿，第一指关节弯曲。初学者肌腱脆弱，慎用。
  • Sloper 斜面：开放手抓圆弧面。手掌小指一侧贴近岩壁，对肌腱最友好。
  • Pocket 洞：两三根手指伸进洞里。Mono 是单指洞，很容易伤。Jib 是很小的凸起，多当脚点，偶尔当救命手。

3.2Edging脚尖踩点 Edging

  • Stand on the inside edge of the forefoot, under the big and second toe — the stiffest rubber.
  • Keep the ankle vertical, like a ballerina. Do not roll it in or out.
  • Look at the foothold first, then place the foot. Never blind-foot.
  • 用前脚掌内侧（大脚趾和二脚趾之间）踩点，那是鞋底最硬、最灵敏的区域。
  • 脚踝尽量直立，像芭蕾，不要内外翻。
  • 先看准脚点再落脚，不要盲踩。

Heel down脚跟下沉

  • After you place the foot, drop the heel so more rubber sits on the hold.
  • Weight goes onto the skeleton and the shoe; hips come into the wall; the arms get a rest.
  • 踩上之后主动把脚跟压下去，让更多橡胶贴住岩点。
  • 体重交给骨骼和鞋底，髋靠近墙，手臂得以休息。

Smear抹点 Smear

  • When there is no distinct foothold, press the whole sole onto the blank wall.
  • Keep the core tight, and keep pushing down and back so friction does not break.
  • 没有明显脚点时，把整只鞋底平贴在光滑岩面上。
  • 核心收紧，持续向下、向后压，才能保住摩擦。
