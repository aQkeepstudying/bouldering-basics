Introduction to Bouldering 抱石基础入门

Before bouldering攀爬前

1.1Gear — choosing climbing shoes装备：选攀岩鞋

  • Size: beginners should go half a size to one size up, or use their street-shoe size. Your toes should touch the front of the shoe comfortably, without intense pain.
  • Why not buy extremely tight shoes? Extreme tightness is for high-precision edging by advanced climbers. Beginners do not yet have that small-foot-muscle strength, and painful shoes will just make you want to quit.
  • What the shoes do: climbing shoes use sticky rubber that gives far more friction than regular sneakers, so you can stand on holds that look impossible in ordinary shoes.
  • 尺码：初学者选大半码到一码，或平日鞋码。脚趾碰到鞋头即可，不要剧痛。
  • 为什么不要买特别紧的鞋？极致贴合是给进阶者做精确踩点用的。初学者脚部小肌群还不够，痛只会让你想放弃。
  • 鞋的作用：攀岩鞋的橡胶摩擦力远高于普通运动鞋，才能踩住看起来不可能的点。

1.2Grades and color分级与颜色

  • American bouldering grades: V0 to V17. The number is only a starting point for how hard a problem feels.
  • European bouldering grades: 4a to 9a.
  • If you are a beginner: start at V0 and add difficulty slowly. Do not jump grades just because a color looks easy.
  • Color vs. grade: holds of the same color are one problem. That color is mapped to a grade, but every gym has its own color chart on the wall — read that chart before you pick a line.
  • 美式抱石等级：V0 到 V17。数字只是难度入口，手感还看点、距离和坡度。
  • 欧式抱石等级：4a 到 9a。
  • 初学者：从 V0 开始，慢慢往上加。不要因为某条颜色看起来简单就跳级。
  • 颜色与等级：同一种颜色是一条线路，颜色会对应等级。每个岩馆有自己的颜色说明图，选线前先看图。

1.3How to start and finish如何开始和结束

  • Read the start tags. Look for tape or labels on the holds. If there is one tag on a hold, both hands must start there. If there are two tags on different holds, one hand goes on each. Some gyms also tag specific starting footholds.
  • To begin, both feet leave the ground from that start.
  • Finish: match both hands on the Top hold and keep them there, stably, for two to three seconds — or tap the top marker, if that is how the gym marks a send.
  • 先看起步标记。找岩点上的胶带或标签。一个点上只有一枚标记：双手都要放在这个点。两个点上各有标记：一手一个。有的岩馆还会标起步脚点。
  • 开始时，双脚离开地面。
  • 结束：双手同时抓稳终点（Top），保持两到三秒；或按该馆规则轻拍顶部标记。

1.4Landing safely安全落地

  • Land on both feet: keep your feet about shoulder-width apart. That gives you a stable base and makes it less likely you will twist an ankle.
  • Bend your knees: let your knees and hips flex the moment you hit the pad. They are the shock absorbers. Do not keep your legs stiff.
  • Tuck your arms: keep your arms close to your chest, like a bear hug. Do not reach out to catch yourself with your hands — that is how wrists get broken.
  • Roll backward: let your momentum carry you into a smooth roll onto your back and shoulders, so leftover energy leaves the body instead of jamming into one joint.
  • 双脚落地：两脚大约与肩同宽，底盘更稳，脚踝不容易扭。
  • 弯曲膝盖：一碰到垫子，膝盖和髋马上屈曲，当作减震。不要把腿绷直。
  • 收臂：手臂靠近胸口，像熊抱。不要伸手去撑地——手腕很容易骨折。
  • 向后滚：顺着惯性滚到后背和肩膀，把剩余力量散掉，而不是砸进某一个关节。

1.5Mindset心态

Bouldering is a dialogue with yourself, not a competition against the person on the next problem.

You are solving a physics puzzle: gravity, the wall, and your own body. Everyone’s body is different, so everyone’s beta (the sequence that works) is different. Copying someone taller or stronger is optional, not a rule.

Know when to quit. If your forearms are pumped, or the drop feels unsafe, come down. Safety always comes first. Getting slightly better, slowly, is the whole game.

攀岩是跟自己对话，不是和旁边那个人比赛。

你在解一道物理题：重力、岩壁、自己的身体。每个人体型不同，过法（beta）也不同。可以看别人，但不必照抄更高或更壮的人。

及时放弃。前臂泵死了，或掉落觉得不安全，就下来。安全永远第一。缓慢进步，就是这件事本身。

1.6Before the real climbing正式攀爬前

  • Nails: check your fingernails and trim them. Long nails snag on holds and tear.
  • Pockets: empty your pockets. Keys, phones, and bottles hurt you — and whoever is under you — if you fall.
  • Warm up: always warm up before you climb. Cold fingers and shoulders are easy to injure.
  • Read the wall: before you pull on, look at the problem — where it goes, where you will land if you fall. Do not climb directly above or below someone else.
  • Pad etiquette: when you finish, step off the mats. Do not walk across the pads; they are a landing zone, not a corridor.
  • 指甲：先检查，最好修剪。长指甲容易卡在岩点上，把自己撕伤。
  • 口袋：不要装东西。钥匙、手机、水瓶掉下去会伤到自己，也会伤到下面的人。
  • 热身：爬之前一定要热身，避免手指和肩关节损伤。
  • 看线：上手前先观察线路走向、掉落会落在哪。不要爬到别人正上方或正下方。
  • 礼仪：结束后离开垫子。最好不要在垫子上走来走去——垫子是落地用的，不是过道。

Basics基本知识 first time on the wall第一次上墙，重建发力

2.1Getting on the wall: like climbing stairs上墙：像走楼梯

  • Route selection: start with the lowest difficulty grade in the gym (for example V0–V1). You are learning how the body works, not chasing a number.
  • Pre-climb observation: before pulling on, look at the problem — where it goes, and where you will land if you fall. Keep the landing zone clear and safe. Do not climb directly above or below anyone.
  • Core effort: once on the wall, put your attention on the feet. Move like walking up stairs: the legs push the body up, while the hands only keep you on the wall and keep you balanced.
  • Practice descent: repeatedly practice getting off the wall safely. Downclimbing is how you start to trust the feet.
  • 选线：从馆里最简单的等级开始（例如 V0–V1）。现在练的是身体怎么发力，不是数字。
  • 上手前先看：看这条线往哪走、掉了会落在哪。垫子上不要有人。也不要爬到别人正上方或正下方。
  • 上了墙：把注意力放在脚上。像走楼梯一样往上：腿把人推上去，手只负责挂住和保持平衡。
  • 反复练习下降：会下来，才敢把重心交给脚。

2.2Why do you get so tired so quickly?为什么这么快就累了

On the ground, our instinct is to “grip tight, pull in, and muscle through.” On a vertical wall, those same land-based instincts make the body lock up and burn out after a few moves.

在陆地上，我们的直觉是“抓紧、靠拢、用力”。上了垂直的岩壁，这套动作往往会让身体迅速僵硬、消耗殆尽。

  1. Your hands aren't for pulling up; your feet are the engine 手不是用来向上拉的，脚才是推进器

    The Trap:beginners instinctively try to pull themselves up with their arms like a pull-up, leading to pumped forearms and exhaustion within a few moves.

    反直觉点：初学者上墙的第一反应往往是像引体向上一样，用手臂拼命把身体往上拉，结果没爬几步就小臂泵血、双臂僵硬。

    The Fix:your legs are the elevator. Every move should be driven by pushing off your feet — like doing a squat or standing up — to elevate the body. Hands only hang on and keep you balanced. Bouldering is fundamentally a lower-body sport.

    需要培养：用双腿发力站起，双手只是用来保持平衡和固定身体。想象你的双腿是电梯，每一次移动都是靠脚踩着点、像蹲起或站立一样把身体推上去。攀岩其实是一项下肢运动。

  2. Keep your pelvis (hips) close to the wall, not your chest 贴墙的是胯，不是胸口

    The Trap:for a sense of security, beginners press their chest and belly flat against the wall like a frog. That pulls the centre of gravity outward and places a massive hanging strain on the arms.

    反直觉点：为了追求安全感，初学者本能地会让胸口、肚子紧紧贴在岩壁上，整个人像一只趴在墙上的青蛙。这种姿势会把重心往外拉，让手臂承受巨大的悬挂拉力。

    The Fix:actively press your hips in. Through side-climbing or opening the hips, bring the pelvis close to the wall so the centre of gravity falls directly over the feet. The closer the hips are to the wall, the more vertical the pressure on the feet, and the lighter the hands feel.

    需要培养：主动把胯部压近岩壁，甚至通过侧身或开胯，让重心落在脚的正上方。骨盆越贴近墙面，双脚对岩点的压力就越垂直，双手的负担就越小。

  3. Straight arms beat bent-elbow bracing 打直手臂比曲肘死撑更省力

    The Trap:when feeling insecure, people unconsciously bend the elbows and shrug the shoulders, locking the body tightly against the wall. This keeps the arm muscles in a constant state of contraction.

    反直觉点：害怕掉落时，人会不自觉地把胳膊肘弯曲、耸起肩膀，把身体紧紧锁在离墙很近的地方。但这会让手臂肌肉一直处于持续收缩的紧张状态。

    The Fix:hang with straight, relaxed arms. Unless you are executing a dynamic move, let the arms hang like two suspension ropes, using bone and joints to support the weight, so the muscles get a chance to rest.

    需要培养：在没有做动态动作时，尽量让手臂像两根吊绳一样自然下垂打直，利用骨骼和关节的挂力来支撑体重，给肌肉争取喘息的时间。

  4. Trust tiny footholds and commit your weight 学会信任微小的脚点，敢于把重心完全交出去

    The Trap:beginners look for massive, comfortable ledges, or try to smear the shoes haphazardly. Facing a small plastic nub, instinct tells you it will slip.

    反直觉点：初学者倾向于把脚踩在很大、很舒服的坑里，或者拼命用鞋底去蹭墙。面对一个光滑的小突起，本能会抗拒把身体重量压上去，觉得这绝对会滑。

    The Fix:trust your rubber. As long as the toe is precisely placed on the hold with vertical downward pressure, even a thumbnail-sized edge is enough to support the entire body.

    需要培养：建立对攀岩鞋橡胶摩擦力的信任。很多时候，只要鞋尖精确地踩在点上，并且保持垂直向下的压力，哪怕只有指甲盖大小的面积也足够支撑全身。

  5. Relaxation is harder than tension: micro-releasing as you climb 放松比发力更难：动态过程中的瞬间卸力

    The Trap:tension causes all muscles to contract at once, leading to stiff, jerky movement and wasted energy even when you are not going anywhere.

    反直觉点：人在紧张时全身肌肉会同时绷紧，导致动作僵硬、卡顿，甚至在不需要发力的时候也在内耗体力。

    The Fix:learn to micro-relax. On a comfortable hold, shake out and relax one hand at a time, and breathe. Explode only during the actual movement, then immediately find a new balance point to unload.

    需要培养：学会在攀爬间隙局部放松。抓到比较好的手点时，单手交替放松、甩手、调整呼吸；移动手脚的瞬间果断发力，移动完成后立刻找到新的平衡点卸力。

2.3Physics that changes the move用物理改动作

  1. Leverage and torque 杠杆与力矩

    The physics:torque is the rotational force caused by gravity pulling you away from the wall. Climbing is the constant work of keeping that rotation from winning.

    物理：力矩是重力把你转离墙的那个力。攀岩就是一直在跟这个转动作对。

    The application:treat the body as a multi-lever system. Most useful techniques are just moving the fulcrum or shortening the lever arm r. When one hand reaches far, the hips tend to swing out. A drop-knee or a hip turn brings the centre of gravity back in, shortens r, and takes a large load off the arms and back.

    用在墙上：把身体看成一个多级杠杆系统。许多技巧，本质都是在挪支点、缩短力臂 r。一只手够远时，髋容易往外摆。侧身或 drop knee 把重心收回来，缩短 r，背和手臂会轻很多。

  2. Action and reaction: channeling force through the skeleton 作用与反作用：让力穿过骨架

    The physics:Newton’s third law. The wall never lifts you. It only gives back the exact force you put into it.

    物理：牛顿第三定律。岩壁不会主动把你托上去，它给予你的永远是你施加给它的力的反向镜像。

    The application:beginners often just squeeze harder. What matters more is the direction of the force at each contact. On an overhang the foot has to push inward, or there is no normal force, and therefore no friction. Direct that reaction through the skeleton, not through a pumped forearm — four ounces moving a thousand pounds.

    用在墙上：初学者容易死抓，只知道用力的大小。更要紧的是每个接触点力的方向。外倾墙上，脚要往里压，才有法向力，也才有摩擦。让这股回力穿过核心骨架，不要穿过已经泵死的小臂，才是四两拨千斤。

  3. Inertia and acceleration: momentum over muscle 惯性与加速度：用动量，不要纯靠肌肉

    The physics:force produces acceleration; speed creates distance. Because of inertia, a moving body can arrive at a hold that is impossible from a dead hang.

    物理：受力才有加速度，有速度才有距离。因为惯性，运动中的身体可以够到静止时够不到的点。

    The application:pure arm-pulling makes long reaches exhausting. Chain a continuous acceleration: toe push → leg drive → hip swing → one-hand catch. Use the momentum generated by the legs to float to the next hold, instead of fighting the gap with the biceps.

    用在墙上：纯靠手拉，远点会把人抽干。做出连续加速：脚尖推 → 腿 → 髋摆 → 单手够。用腿带出来的动量荡过去，不要靠二头肌硬够。

2.4What actually saves energy怎样更省力

  1. Full-body integration: less pressure for your arm 全身连成一根：让手臂更轻
    • Toe placement: like a ballet dancer pointing onto the hold. A small, precise contact gives a clean upward push, so the arms do less hanging.
    • Straight arms: a bent elbow becomes a new fulcrum. You are no longer one crane from the feet to the hands, so the biceps have to fight gravity on their own, and the forearms pump.
    • Close to the wall: hips in shortens the lever arm of your body weight against the hands. Same person, much less pull at the arms.
    • 脚尖踩点：像芭蕾踩在点上。接触面小而准，更容易找到向上的正压力，手臂少做吊着的活。
    • 手臂打直：弯肘会多出一个支点。身体不再是从脚到手的一根杆，二头肌只好自己跟重力较劲，然后小臂泵死。
    • 贴进岩壁：髋靠近墙，缩短体重对手的力臂。越贴近岩壁，手臂压力越小。
  2. Static balance on the wall 墙上的平衡
    • Two-point, opposite side: diagonal limbs (right hand + left foot) give the most stable pair, and resist the rotation that tries to peel you off. Same-side (left hand + left foot) lets the body rotate away from the wall.
    • Three-point, the stable triangle: two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand. The free limb is the one that moves.
    • 两点、异侧：对角的手脚（右手+左脚）更稳，更能顶住把人转离墙的力矩。同侧（左手+左脚）旋转力矩更大，更容易被转出去。
    • 三点、稳定的三角：两手一脚，或两脚一手。空出来的那只手脚去够下一个点。
  3. Dynamic flow: moving from one balance to the next 前进就是从一个平衡走到下一个

    Five seconds before you move:

    移动前的五秒，想这三件事：

    • Hold direction: which way does the next hold face? Match the hand to that orientation.
    • Assess stability: if you release this hand, is what remains a triangle or an opposite-side pair — or will you rotate off?
    • Adjust, then execute: if it would rotate, high-step or switch feet first. Once the feet are set, explode off the legs and use that momentum to catch the next hold.
    • 看开口：下一个点朝哪开？开口朝右，右手更顺；点在左侧，左手更顺。
    • 松手之前：松掉这只手之后，剩下的是三角或异侧，还是会被转出去？
    • 调整再动：若会被转出去，先上高脚或换脚。脚放稳了再蹬，用脚蹬出来的动能和惯性够到下一个点。

Skills技巧 handholds and footwork抓法与脚法

3.1Handholds岩点与抓法

  • Jug: a big, positive hold. Wrap fingers and palm around it — the standard grip.
  • Pinch: squeeze the hold between the thumb and the other four fingers, like a volume or a rail.
  • Crimp: fingertips on a small edge, first knuckle flexed. Beginners’ tendons are fragile — use this cautiously.
  • Sloper: an open-hand on a rounded surface. Keep the pinky side of the palm close to the wall; this is the kindest grip for tendons.
  • Pocket: two or three fingers in a hole. Mono is a one-finger pocket — easy to injure. Jib: a tiny chip; often a foot, sometimes a desperate hand.
  • Jug 深点：好抓的大点。手指和手掌包住，就是标准的握。
  • Pinch 捏点：拇指对另外四指，把体积块或横条捏住。
  • Crimp 抠点：指尖抠小沿，第一指关节弯曲。初学者肌腱脆弱，慎用。
  • Sloper 斜面：开放手抓圆弧面。手掌小指一侧贴近岩壁，对肌腱最友好。
  • Pocket 洞：两三根手指伸进洞里。Mono 是单指洞，很容易伤。Jib 是很小的凸起，多当脚点，偶尔当救命手。

3.2Edging脚尖踩点 Edging

  • Stand on the inside edge of the forefoot, under the big and second toe — the stiffest rubber.
  • Keep the ankle vertical, like a ballerina. Do not roll it in or out.
  • Look at the foothold first, then place the foot. Never blind-foot.
  • 用前脚掌内侧（大脚趾和二脚趾之间）踩点，那是鞋底最硬、最灵敏的区域。
  • 脚踝尽量直立，像芭蕾，不要内外翻。
  • 先看准脚点再落脚，不要盲踩。

Heel down脚跟下沉

  • After you place the foot, drop the heel so more rubber sits on the hold.
  • Weight goes onto the skeleton and the shoe; hips come into the wall; the arms get a rest.
  • 踩上之后主动把脚跟压下去，让更多橡胶贴住岩点。
  • 体重交给骨骼和鞋底，髋靠近墙，手臂得以休息。

Smear抹点 Smear

  • When there is no distinct foothold, press the whole sole onto the blank wall.
  • Keep the core tight, and keep pushing down and back so friction does not break.
  • 没有明显脚点时，把整只鞋底平贴在光滑岩面上。
  • 核心收紧，持续向下、向后压，才能保住摩擦。
